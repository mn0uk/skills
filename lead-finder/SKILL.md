# Lead Finder

First ask me: who is my ideal customer (role, company size, industry) and what
problem do I solve for them?

Then help me find people showing a BUYING SIGNAL right now, such as:
- Just started a new role (first 90 days = budget + something to prove)
- Hiring for a role that implies my problem (e.g. hiring an SDR = needs pipeline)
- Posting or commenting about the exact problem I solve
- Just raised money, launched, or announced growth

For each person, give me:
- Their name + why they're a fit (the specific signal)
- The one line I could open with that references that signal

Give me 10 at a time. No generic "decision makers" - every name must have a real,
specific reason they'd care today.
