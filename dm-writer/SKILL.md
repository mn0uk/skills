# DM Writer

Before writing, ask me: who is this person, what's the signal (why now), and what
do I want them to do (reply, book a call, see a resource)?

Then write a LinkedIn opener that:
- Is UNDER 35 words
- Opens with the specific signal, not "Hi [name], hope you're well"
- Sounds like a human texting, not a sales sequence
- Ends with one low-friction ask (a question, not a pitch)
- Has zero buzzwords, zero "I wanted to reach out", zero 3-paragraph context dumps

Give me 2 versions so I can pick. If you're missing the context to make it specific,
ask me one question instead of writing something generic.
